1. Our messaging programs

HeadingOver runs two text messaging programs.

Program 1: HeadingOver Assistant

Program name: HeadingOver Assistant

Description: HeadingOver is an AI calendar assistant. After you sign up, you text the HeadingOver number you were given to add, change, or find events on your connected Google Calendar. HeadingOver texts you back with calendar confirmations, follow-up questions when a request is unclear, reminders you set, an optional daily summary, and account notices.

Program 2: Crew Appointment Texts

Program name: Appointment confirmations and reminders from home-service crews, via HeadingOver

Description: Small home-service businesses ("crews"), such as pressure washing, lawn care, cleaning, pet waste removal, and handyman services, use HeadingOver to send their own customers appointment confirmations, reminders, and scheduling updates. Every message starts with the crew's business name followed by "via HeadingOver." These messages are informational only and never contain marketing. Each crew's messaging program may be registered with carriers under that crew's own business name.

2. How you opt in

  • HeadingOver Assistant: You opt in by entering your mobile number and checking the SMS consent box when you sign up on headingover.com. Because texting is how the Assistant works, you need to agree to receive these texts to use it by text.
  • Crew Appointment Texts: You opt in by giving your mobile number to a crew that uses HeadingOver and agreeing to receive appointment texts from that crew, for example by checking a box on its booking form or agreeing when you book by phone. The crew is responsible for getting your consent and keeping a record of it. Agreeing to receive these texts is not a condition of buying any goods or services.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies. For the HeadingOver Assistant, you receive replies to your own messages, the reminders you set, and up to one daily summary per day if you turn it on. For Crew Appointment Texts, you typically receive 1 to 4 messages per appointment.

4. Cost

Msg & data rates may apply. HeadingOver does not charge you for receiving text messages, but your wireless carrier's standard message and data rates may apply. Paid HeadingOver plans are billed separately, as described in our Terms of Service.

5. How to opt out (STOP)

You can stop text messages at any time. Reply STOP to the number that texted you. We also accept QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, and STOPALL, and we will honor any other reasonable request to stop, such as replying "stop texting me" or emailing hello@headingover.com.

After you opt out, we will send one final text confirming that you are unsubscribed, and you will not receive further texts from that program unless you opt in again. To opt back in, reply START or UNSTOP.

How STOP applies:

  • A STOP sent to one crew's appointment texts stops texts from that crew only. It does not stop texts from other crews that use HeadingOver, or from the HeadingOver Assistant.
  • A STOP sent to the HeadingOver Assistant stops Assistant texts. Because texting is how the Assistant works, you won't be able to use it by text until you opt back in.
  • Opting out of texts does not cancel a paid plan. Cancel your plan as described in our Terms of Service.
  • Opting out of a crew's texts does not cancel your appointment. Contact the crew directly to change or cancel an appointment.

6. Help (HELP)

Reply HELP to the number that texted you for help, email us at hello@headingover.com, or visit https://headingover.com.

7. Quiet hours

Crew Appointment Texts are sent only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. in the recipient's local time. HeadingOver Assistant replies are sent when you text us, and reminders you set are sent at the times you choose.

10. Responsibilities of crews

Crews that use HeadingOver to text their customers agree, under our Terms of Service, to:

  • obtain each customer's consent to receive appointment texts before the first text is sent, and keep a record of it (verbal consent must be logged in HeadingOver);

  • honor every opt-out; and

HeadingOver automatically identifies the crew by name in every customer text, includes opt-out instructions, follows the quiet hours above, and processes STOP and HELP replies.
