HeadingOver Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026
Last updated: November 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HeadingOver LLC, a Utah limited liability company ("HeadingOver," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit headingover.com (the "Site"), join our waitlist, or use the HeadingOver calendar assistant by text message (SMS), phone call, or the web (together, the "Service").

HeadingOver is an AI calendar assistant. You text or call HeadingOver in plain language (for example, "Book the Smiths Tuesday at 3, pressure wash" or "Practice Tuesdays at 4, remind me the night before"), and it adds, changes, or finds events on the Google Calendar you connect. Home-service crews can also have HeadingOver send appointment confirmations and reminders to their own customers.

Questions? Email us at hello@headingover.com.

1. Summary

  • We collect what we need to run the Service: your contact information, your phone number, the texts and calls you send us, and the Google Calendar data you let us access.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.
  • We share information only with service providers that help us run the Service (such as Twilio, Google, Anthropic, Stripe, and our hosting providers), and only as needed.
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes.
  • Our use of Google Calendar data follows Google's API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your data at any time.
  • Account holders must be 18 or older. Team members aged 16 or 17 may use HeadingOver only through a business account managed by an adult. No one under 16 may use HeadingOver.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Waitlist information. When you join the waitlist, we collect your name, email address, business name, trade (for example, lawn care or cleaning), crew size, and company website.
  • Account information. When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, mobile phone number, time zone, and preferences (for example, whether you want a daily summary text).
  • Texts. When you text HeadingOver, we receive your phone number and the content of your messages.
  • Calls. When you call HeadingOver, we receive your phone number and the audio of the call, which is converted to text (speech-to-text) so the assistant can understand you. At the start of each call, the assistant tells you it is an AI and that the call is transcribed. We keep call audio only as long as needed to transcribe and handle your request, and we keep any recordings and transcripts only as described in Section 8.
  • Crew (business) account information. If you use HeadingOver for a business, we collect your business name, your team members' names and phone numbers, and the customer information you or your team give us, such as customer names, phone numbers, service addresses, appointment times, and job details.
  • Payment information. If you buy a paid plan, our payment processor, Stripe, collects your payment card details. We do not store your full card number. We receive limited details such as your billing name, card type, the last four digits of your card, and your subscription status.
  • Support communications. If you email us for help, we keep those messages so we can respond.

2.2 Google Calendar data

When you connect your Google account, you choose to give HeadingOver permission to access your Google Calendar. With that permission, HeadingOver:

  • reads your existing events (such as titles, dates, times, durations, recurrence, locations, descriptions, reminders, and attendees) to check for conflicts, find events you want to change, and send you schedule summaries and reminders; and
  • creates and updates events you ask for.

We also receive basic Google account information (such as your name and email address) to identify your account. We request only the Google permissions needed for these features. See Section 6 for how we handle Google user data.

2.3 Information collected automatically

  • Usage data. Records of how you use the Service, such as when messages were sent, which actions were taken (for example, "event created"), message counts (used for plan limits and billing), and error logs.
  • Site data. When you visit the Site, our website host (Netlify) and our systems may receive your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring page, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Cookies. The Site uses only cookies and similar technologies needed for the Site to work. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

2.4 Information about other people

When you ask HeadingOver to schedule something involving another person (for example, a customer or an event attendee), you may give us that person's name, phone number, or other details. Please only share information you have the right to share. Crews must follow the customer consent rules in our Terms of Service (https://headingover.com/terms) and SMS Terms (https://headingover.com/sms-terms).

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  1. Provide the Service, including understanding your texts and calls, checking your calendar for conflicts, creating and updating events, and sending you confirmations, reminders you set, optional daily summaries, and account notices.
  2. Send customer texts for crews, such as appointment confirmations and reminders, on the crew's behalf and at its direction.
  3. Ask follow-up questions when your request is unclear.
  4. Manage accounts, subscriptions, and billing.
  5. Provide support and respond to your questions.
  6. Keep the Service safe and working, including preventing fraud, spam, and abuse, fixing bugs, and monitoring errors.
  7. Improve the Service, for example by reviewing error reports and aggregated usage trends, subject to the limits on Google user data in Section 6.
  8. Communicate with you about the Service, such as waitlist updates, launch news, changes to our terms, and account or security notices. You can opt out of non-essential emails at any time.
  9. Comply with the law and enforce our terms.

We do not use your information for targeted or interest-based advertising.

4. AI processing

HeadingOver uses an artificial intelligence (AI) model provided by Anthropic to read your messages (and call transcripts) and work out what you want, such as the event title, date, time, duration, recurrence, reminders, and any attendee or customer phone number.

  • We send Anthropic only the information needed to handle your request, which may include your message, relevant details of existing calendar events (for example, to check conflicts or find the event to change), and your time zone.
  • Under its commercial terms with us, Anthropic is not permitted to use our customers' data to train its models.
  • AI can make mistakes. Always check your calendar entries. See our Terms of Service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising.

If HeadingOver is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction. Any successor must continue to honor this Privacy Policy for the information it receives, including the Google user data and SMS commitments below, or give you notice and a choice.

6. Google API Services: how we use Google user data

HeadingOver's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy (https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  1. Used only to provide the Service. We use Google Calendar data only to provide and improve the user-facing features of HeadingOver: reading existing events to check conflicts, find events you want to change, and send schedule summaries and reminders; creating and updating events you ask for; and, for crews, sending appointment confirmations and reminders to customers.
  2. Transfers only to provide the Service. We do not transfer Google user data to others except as needed to provide or improve these user-facing features (for example, to our hosting, database, texting, and AI providers acting on our behalf, including sending relevant event details to Anthropic for AI processing), to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets with notice to you.
  3. No advertising. We do not use or transfer Google user data to serve ads, including retargeting, personalized, or interest-based ads.
  4. No selling. We do not sell Google user data, and we do not transfer it to data brokers or information resellers.
  5. No human reading, except in limited cases. No person at HeadingOver reads your Google user data unless: (a) you give us permission for specific events or messages (for example, when you ask for help with a problem); (b) it is necessary for security purposes, such as investigating abuse; (c) it is necessary to comply with applicable law; or (d) the data has been aggregated and anonymized and is used for internal operations as allowed by applicable law.
  6. No AI model training. We do not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models, and our AI provider, Anthropic, is not permitted to train its models on it.
  7. No credit or lending decisions. We do not use Google user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Disconnecting Google. You can disconnect HeadingOver from your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by emailing hello@headingover.com. When you disconnect, we stop accessing your calendar and delete your stored Google access tokens within 7 days. Events HeadingOver already placed on your calendar stay there unless you delete them.

7. Text messages and phone calls

  • Consent. We send text messages only to people who agreed to receive them, or, for appointment reminders, to customers who agreed to receive texts from the crew that booked them. See our SMS Terms (https://headingover.com/sms-terms).
  • Opt out. Reply STOP to any HeadingOver text to stop receiving texts from that program. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply.
  • No sharing for marketing. No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
  • Calls. When you call HeadingOver, you are talking to an automated AI assistant. At the start of each call, it tells you it is an AI and that the call is transcribed.

9. How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, such as encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), encryption of stored Google access tokens, access controls that limit who can reach production data, and keeping secret keys out of our code. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law, including Utah's data breach notification law.

10. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • Access the personal information we have about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong.
  • Delete your account and personal information.
  • Get a copy of your information in a portable format.
  • Opt out of texts (reply STOP) and of non-essential emails (use the unsubscribe link).
  • Disconnect Google at any time (see Section 6).

To make a request, email hello@headingover.com from the email address on your account. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will respond within 45 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

If you are a customer of a crew that uses HeadingOver to send you appointment texts, that crew controls your information. You can reply STOP to stop the texts, and you can contact the crew or email us, and we will help.

11. Utah residents and AI disclosure

HeadingOver is based in Utah. Based on our current size, the Utah Consumer Privacy Act does not apply to us, but we voluntarily offer the rights described in Section 10 to everyone, and we do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

HeadingOver uses generative AI. We tell you clearly, up front, that you are interacting with an AI assistant, and that label stays in place throughout your use of the Service, including at the start of every call.

Team members aged 16 or 17 may use HeadingOver only through a business account managed by an adult account holder. If we learn we have collected information from someone under 16, we will delete it promptly. If you believe someone under 16 has given us information, contact us at hello@headingover.com.
