Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Do my techs need to download an app?

No. You text or call HeadingOver. Your crew gets a text.

Will you text my customers?

Only if they have agreed to receive texts from your business. Confirmations and reminders go out with their consent. They can reply STOP to opt out.

How much does it cost?

Founding pilot: 30 days free, then $49/month locked for 12 months, for the first 5 St. George crews. Regular Crew plan: $59/month for up to 3 users and 300 texts ($0.05 per extra text). We'll confirm before any charge, and you can cancel online anytime.

What can I text or call in today?

Jobs, shifts, and time-off changes you make as the manager. HeadingOver adds them to your Google Calendar and texts the people who need to know.

What is a founding pilot?

An early spot for a crew manager to use HeadingOver on real jobs. Pilot crews help pick what’s next.

Already using Jobber or a whiteboard?

Keep it. HeadingOver lets you change your schedule by text from the truck, and your calendar and crew update in seconds. During the pilot we'll work with how you already schedule.

Does it work with the calendar I already use?

It works with Google Calendar today. You connect it once. HeadingOver uses your Google Calendar only to add and update the events you ask for and to check your schedule for conflicts. We never sell your calendar data or use it to train AI.
