Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Do my techs need to download an app?

No. You’ll text HeadingOver. Texts to your crew are coming soon.

Will you text my customers?

Not at launch. HeadingOver will text you. Texts to your crew are coming soon. Customer reminders come later, and only to customers who have agreed to texts from your business.

How much does it cost?

Early access: First 30 days free once crew texts are on. No card, nobody charged before then. Then $49/month locked for 12 months, for crews of up to 6 and the first 5 crews only. After early access, Crew is $59/month for up to 3 users (300 texts to HeadingOver a month, plus $0.05 per extra text), and Team is $99/month for 4 to 8 users (600 texts to HeadingOver a month, plus $0.05 per extra text). You'll see your exact price before your first charge, get a reminder before it, and can cancel online anytime.

What will I be able to text in?

Jobs, shifts, and time-off changes you make as the manager. HeadingOver will add them to your Google Calendar. Texts to your crew are coming soon.

What is early access?

The first 5 crews get it first, free for 30 days once crew texts are on, then $49/mo locked for 12 months for crews up to 6.

Already using Jobber?

HeadingOver is built for Google Calendar, so it will be best for crews who schedule there or on a whiteboard. If your schedule lives in another app, tell us. Early access crews help pick what we connect next.

Will it work with the calendar I already use?

It is built to work with Google Calendar. You’ll connect it once. HeadingOver uses your Google Calendar only to add and update the events you ask for and to check your schedule for conflicts. We never sell your calendar data or use it to train AI.
