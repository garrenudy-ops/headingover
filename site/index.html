Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Do I need to download an app?

No. You get a phone number to text or call. Your jobs show up in the Google Calendar app you already have.

What if it gets a job wrong?

It reads every booking back to you, and if anything is unclear, like a missing time or address, it texts you a quick question before adding it. You can fix anything by replying in plain words.

Will my customers know it's automated?

Texts are signed with your business name and written to sound like you. When a customer replies with something other than confirm or reschedule, it gets passed to you.

Does it work with the calendar I already use?

It works with Google Calendar today. You connect it once, and HeadingOver only touches the events it creates or that you ask it to change.

Can customers stop the texts?

Yes. Any customer can reply STOP and they won't get any more texts. We only text customers you've booked.

When can I start using it?

We're opening to a small group of crews first. Join the waitlist and we'll reach out to set you up and hear how you book jobs today.
